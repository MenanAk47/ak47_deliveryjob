## 🔥 Key Features

Designed to improve roleplay immersion and economy balancing:

### 📦 Interactive Dispatch & Career Progression

* **Custom LS Express Tablet UI:** Sleek and responsive NUI dispatch board showing driver name, current rank, XP progress bar, active job statuses, and available delivery routes.
* **10-Tier Rank System:** Earn persistent delivery XP to climb the corporate ladder from Trainee up to Regional Director, unlocking higher-capacity commercial vehicles (Pony, Speedo, Mule, Burrito, Benson).
* **Optional Job Whitelist:** Toggle freely between an open public job or a restricted job grade via `config.lua`.

### 🚚 Standard Delivery Routes & Randomized Logistics

* **Dynamic Multi-Stop Deliveries:** Tackle standard routes across LS (Davis Residential, Vespucci Beach, Rockford Hills, Business Bulk, Bank Transport, and Nikola Ave).
* **Randomized Route Sequences:** Delivery stops are automatically randomized using server-side shuffling to ensure every shift feels fresh and unpredictable.
* **Realistic Customer Drop-offs:** Dynamic recipient NPCs spawn on delivery locations with lifelike mobile phone animations to receive parcels.

### ⚡ High-Stakes On-Demand Express Deliveries

* **Urgent Timed Contracts:** Generated dynamically with custom destination spots, high base payouts, and tight countdown timers.
* **Package Condition & Damage Physics:** High-speed driving (>110 MPH) and vehicle collisions degrade package health in real time.
* **Pristine Condition Bonus:** Delivering intact packages (100% condition) rewards couriers with a lucrative 25%–30% bonus payout.

### 📊 Shift Tracking & Immersive Interactions

* **Dynamic In-Game HUD:** Clean minimal HUD widgets display live stop addresses, remaining drops, express countdown timers, and color-coded cargo condition bars.
* **Physical Package Handling:** Couriers must walk to the rear of their delivery vehicle to grab packages, carry synced box props with realistic carry animations, and deliver them to customers.
* **End-of-Shift Summary:** Clocking out at the depot presents a polished shift report displaying total shift earnings, routes completed, express jobs completed, and overall XP gains.

### 🛠️ Technical Excellence

* **Multi-Framework & Target Support:** Seamlessly works with ESX, QB, QBX, and various target systems (ox_target, qb-target, qtarget).
* **Highly Secure:** Built-in server-side exploit checks, including distance validation and trigger authentication, to prevent cheating.
* **Automated Fleet & Economy Balancing:** Automatically handles vehicle spawning, license plate generation, keys, fuel initialization, and per-player route cooldowns.

* Add items based on your framework instruction.
* Add item images into inventory.
* Start the script in your server.cfg below framework files.
* Make sure you have all dependencies.
* Restart the server.
